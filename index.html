ChargeWorth

The EV is $11k more on the lot but $2k cheaper every year you drive it. Does it ever catch up - and when? Plug in your numbers.

Run my numbers

Enter your annual kilometers and both cars' prices, consumption, energy costs and service. ChargeWorth computes total cost of ownership year by year, finds the exact break-even year (or tells you honestly when driving alone never closes the gap), and draws both cumulative cost curves with the crossing point marked. Everything runs in your browser.
